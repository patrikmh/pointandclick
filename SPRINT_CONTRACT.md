# Sprint Contracts

## Sprint 1: Volumetric two-round shadow sculpture

### Build Criteria
- [ ] **C1 — Genuinely volumetric visible pieces.** Every round presents at least three separate organic/junk sculpture pieces between viewer/light and wall. Every piece has measurable non-zero depth with at least four non-coplanar surface points and multiple oriented surfaces. At rest and after rotation, the canvas shows lit and shaded surfaces, thickness, foreshortening, and occlusion; no visible piece is itself a seagull or sailboat cutout.
- [ ] **C2 — Selectable two-axis manipulation.** Visible controls let the player select every piece, identify the active piece, and rotate it about two axes. A controlled input changes only the active piece’s pose, changes its rendered pixels and shadow contribution, and leaves the other poses unchanged. Closing/reopening cannot leave held keys or duplicate listeners active.
- [ ] **C3 — One crisp, jointly produced shadow.** At each declared solution, the thresholded wall-shadow mask has exactly one 8-connected foreground component and visually reads as one crisp silhouette, without detached per-object shadow patches or a diffuse halo wider than three canvas pixels at a high-contrast edge. Removing any one piece or evaluating any piece alone fails. Completion uses a bidirectional shape-quality measure (or equivalent missing-area and spill bounds), so merely covering the target with an oversized shape fails.
- [ ] **C4 — Exact target order and progression.** The production round data and visible Swedish copy identify exactly two rounds in this order: `seagull` / one seagull outline, then `sailboat` / one sailboat outline. No starfish target or old anchor/shell/driftwood target hint remains in the sheep game. Only round-1 success opens round 2; only round-2 success closes the game and invokes the existing sheep reveal once.
- [ ] **C5 — Demonstrable solvability.** Each round declares a legal scramble pose and at least one legal solution pose for all pieces. The production matcher rejects the scramble and every single-piece/one-piece-removed case, accepts the solution, and the normal input/completion path can move from the scramble to the solution and advance exactly once per round without direct mutation of round-complete or sheep-revealed state.
- [ ] **C6 — Automated regression contract.** `test/sheep-game.test.mjs` deterministically tests C1–C5 plus teardown on close/reset and does not rely on random success, screenshots, network access, wall-clock flakiness, or a second rendering implementation. `node --test test/sheep-game.test.mjs` exits 0. `npm test` shows no new failure relative to the recorded baseline of one unrelated `missing moon chat option` failure.
- [ ] **C7 — Browser integration and visual evidence.** With `npm start`, a 1440×900 Chromium session reaches the sheep mini-game through the existing loaded-flashlight/cave integration, manipulates visible controls, solves seagull then sailboat, and reaches the existing sheep reveal. Evidence includes reference and game screenshots plus pose/state and console logs; page-error count and console-error count are both zero for open, interaction, transition, finish, close, and reopen.
